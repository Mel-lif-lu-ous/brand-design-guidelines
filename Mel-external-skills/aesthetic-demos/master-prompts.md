# Master Prompts — Frontend Design Pro Demo (11 Aesthetics)

Source: extracted verbatim from the "Master Prompt" callouts in `demos-v02/index.html` of the
[claudekit/frontend-design-pro-demo](https://github.com/claudekit/frontend-design-pro-demo) repo,
plus the companion keyword table from `skills/frontend-design-pro/SKILL.md` in the same repo.

See `adaptation-notes.md` in this folder for how these are meant (and not meant) to be used.

---

## 01 — Minimalism & Swiss Style
File: `swiss-minimalism.html`

**Master prompt:** rigorous grid systems, generous whitespace, massive typography — Monochrome palette with one bold accent. Characterful grotesque sans-serif, asymmetric magazine layout, sharp corners, thin 1px borders, razor-sharp hierarchy.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** clean, swiss, grid-based, generous whitespace, typography-first
**Palette ideas:** Monochrome + one bold accent, beige/gray neutrals
**Signature effects:** Razor-sharp hierarchy, subtle hover lifts, micro-animations, perfect alignment

---

## 02 — Neumorphism
File: `neumorphism.html`

**Master prompt:** monochromatic palette, extruded elements, multiple drop shadows — Elements appear pushed out from the surface using soft inner/outer shadows for depth. Rounded sans-serif fonts, pill-shaped buttons that look "pressed in" when active.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** soft ui, embossed, concave/convex, subtle depth, monochromatic
**Palette ideas:** Single pastel + light/dark variations
**Signature effects:** Multi-layer soft shadows, press/release animations, no hard borders

---

## 03 — Glassmorphism
File: `glassmorphism.html`

**Master prompt:** dark vibrant background, animated mesh gradients, frosted glass cards — UI on backdrop-filter blur with semi-transparent white, 1px white borders. Deep void background with neon accents (Purple, Cyan, Magenta), floating layers.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** frosted glass, translucent, vibrant backdrop, blur, layered
**Palette ideas:** Aurora/sunset backgrounds + semi-transparent whites
**Signature effects:** backdrop-filter: blur(), glowing borders, light reflections, floating layers

---

## 04 — Brutalism
File: `brutalism.html`

**Master prompt:** raw, aggressive, high-contrast aesthetics — Thick black borders (3-4px), hard drop shadows, clashing colors (Neon Yellow on Black). Monospace mixed with display fonts, masonry/broken grid layout, exposed structure.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** raw, unpolished, asymmetric, high contrast, intentionally ugly
**Palette ideas:** Harsh primaries, black/white, occasional neon
**Signature effects:** Sharp corners, huge bold text, exposed grid, "broken" aesthetic

---

## 05 — Claymorphism
File: `claymorphism.html`

**Master prompt:** inflated 3D clay, marshmallow shapes floating in space — Candy pastels (Mint, Pink, Lilac) on cream background. Bouncy rounded typography, floating 3D elements, inner + outer shadows, squishy press effects.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** clay, chunky 3D, toy-like, bubbly, double shadows, pastel
**Palette ideas:** Candy pastels, soft gradients
**Signature effects:** Inner + outer shadows, squishy press effects, oversized rounded elements

---

## 06 — Aurora / Mesh Gradient
File: `aurora-mesh-gradient.html`

**Master prompt:** slow-moving breathing blobs of color (Teal, Violet, Magenta) — Ethereal mesh gradient effect. High-contrast serif paired with clean sans, floating glass-like overlays, subtle breathing animations, layered translucency.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** aurora, northern lights, mesh gradient, luminous, flowing
**Palette ideas:** Teal → purple → pink smooth blends
**Signature effects:** Animated/static CSS or SVG mesh gradients, subtle color breathing, layered translucency

---

## 07 — Retro-Futurism / Cyberpunk
File: `retro-futurism-cyberpunk.html`

**Master prompt:** deep black with aggressive neon accents (Cyan, Hot Pink, Acid Green) — CRT scanlines, glitch text effects, HUD elements. Futuristic display fonts, angular corners, glowing borders, chromatic aberration.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** vaporwave, 80s sci-fi, crt scanlines, neon glow, glitch, chrome
**Palette ideas:** Neon cyan/magenta on deep black, chrome accents
**Signature effects:** Scanlines, chromatic aberration, glitch transitions, long glowing shadows

---

## 08 — 3D Hyperrealism
File: `3d-hyperrealism.html`

**Master prompt:** realistic textures (Brushed Titanium, Carbon Fiber, Optical Glass) — Cinematic lighting with Gunmetal Grey/Silver palette. Technical wide sans-serif, skeuomorphic switches, lens flares, physics-based motion.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** realistic textures, skeuomorphic, metallic, WebGL, tactile
**Palette ideas:** Rich metallics, deep gradients
**Signature effects:** Three.js / CSS 3D, physics-based motion, realistic lighting & reflections

---

## 09 — Vibrant Block / Maximalist
File: `vibrant-block-maximalist.html`

**Master prompt:** solid clashing blocks of pure RGB colors, thick 4px black borders — Massive heavy display fonts, masonry grids, marquee scrolling, instant "snap" hover effects. High-energy, no subtlety.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** bold blocks, duotone, high contrast, geometric, energetic
**Palette ideas:** Complementary/triadic brights, neon on dark
**Signature effects:** Large colorful sections, scroll-snap, dramatic hover scales, animated patterns

---

## 10 — Dark OLED Luxury
File: `dark-oled-luxury.html`

**Master prompt:** absolute black backgrounds with Gold accents, silent premium aesthetic — Subtle noise textures. Regal serif paired with geometric sans, spotlight cursor effects, gold foil text gradients, cinematic entrances.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** deep black, oled-optimized, subtle glow, premium, cinematic
**Palette ideas:** #000000 + vibrant accents (emerald, amber, electric blue)
**Signature effects:** Minimal glows, velvet textures, cinematic entrances, reduced-motion support

---

## 11 — Organic / Biomorphic
File: `organic-biomorphic.html`

**Master prompt:** fluid morphing blob shapes, "Living Earth" palette (Sage Green, Clay, Sand, Moss) — Reject straight lines. Friendly rounded sans, complex border-radius, wavy dividers, breathing motion, gooey effects.

**Keywords (SKILL.md):** fluid shapes, blobs, curved, nature-inspired, hand-drawn
**Palette ideas:** Earthy or muted pastels
**Signature effects:** SVG morphing, gooey effects, irregular borders, gentle spring animations
