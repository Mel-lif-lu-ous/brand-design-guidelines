01 / Principles

Philosophy

Four ideas that hold the visual language together — from album artwork to digital archive to performance context. Each reflects the way the music itself is made: by accumulation, restraint, and attention to what lingers.

01
Sound earns the space
Silence is not empty — it is where the sound lives. Every layout decision defers to the content’s need to breathe. Negative space is presence.
02
Memory as material
Archive and fragment are design primitives here. Glass jars, field recordings, old letters — the system honours the vessel as much as its contents.
03
Nature before abstraction
Textures and tones come from real places: rice fields in Itoshima, frozen walls in Iceland, forest floors at dusk. The palette is grounded, never invented.
04
One layer at a time
Like building a loop, the visual system builds by accumulation. Restraint at each step keeps the whole coherent. Italic earns its moment; colour earns its weight.
02 / Mood Board

Photography

Photography reads as atmospheric and tactile: natural light on water, frost on glass, objects held in hands. Colour carries the residency locations — Iceland’s blue-grey winter, Japan’s green-gold fields, the dark warmth of a studio at night. Never staged, never stock.

03 / Icons

Icons

Line as gesture: 1.5px stroke, rounded joins, no fill. A 20×20 grid with 2px inset — room for a breath. Forest or Sage on light surfaces; Mist where the ground goes dark.

12 / Buttons

Button Variants

The primary button earns its dark fill once per view: a single moment of direction. When it is present, it is the only invitation. 8px radius: grounded, not perfectly round. The pill shape belongs to things that label, never to things that ask you to move.

13 / Navigation

Header

A header that stays as the page moves: wordmark anchored left, links in the centre, one ghost and one dark action on the right. 12px radius throughout. An active page underlines quietly — it does not fill to announce itself.

14 / Cards

Content Cards

Cards begin at rest: hairline border, no weight, no shadow. Hover brings them forward, slowly. The feature card carries emphasis through darkness rather than scale. The gradient shell appears rarely, only where a single piece of work needs to hold the full frame.

15 / Badges

Badges & Tags

Small signals for a layered taxonomy. Dark-filled for version markers, forest-washed for featured work, hairline for general context. A living dot marks what is available now. Badges name and locate — they carry no action of their own.

16 / Forms

Inputs & Controls

Inputs wait quietly until touched. A forest ring arrives on focus — the same clarity that governs buttons. Toggles and sliders hold their active state in that same shade: a consistency between choosing and doing.
